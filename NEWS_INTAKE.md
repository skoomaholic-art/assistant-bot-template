This is a **planned editorial intake process**, not active automatic Gmail synchronization to GitHub.

## Purpose
Use reputable public, permissioned cinema and TV series newsletters as optional inputs into content ideation for `@MyBrand`.

- An initial Gmail subject-line check on 2026-10-28 found film/TV promotional emails but **no obvious messages from** Variety, Deadline, Hollywood Reporter, Screen Rant or IndieWire in the last 180 days. No new subscriptions created.

## Confidentiality boundary
Do **not** use private work-related correspondence, internal EPG schedules, unpublished announcements, attachments, client communications, or confidential promotional planning as source material for the public Instagram account. Publicly verifiable announcements only.

## Editorial flow
1. Read incoming public newsletter items and links.
2. Filter: cinema, series, official streaming release announcements, creator interviews, thoughtful original observations.
3. Independently verify claims on the public publisher/studio site before external publication. Date-sensitive claims must be checked near publication.
4. Save only non-sensitive editorial summaries in `instagram-growth-manager`: source domain, public URL, publication date, topic, one-paragraph authored summary, fact-check status, content hypothesis.
5. Route shortlisted drafts for approval through @your_bot.
6. Monitor results in Viral Lab; never represent newsletter content as original reporting, and do not reproduce long copyrighted passages.

## No unapproved external actions
Subscriptions send the owner's email address to a provider. Subscribe only to named sources approved by the owner, using lawful official forms and links. Never sign up to paid lists or offer a payment method. No bulk mass subscriptions.

## Suggested sources to consider
Official studio/streaming pressrooms and well-established film/TV trade coverage; source selection must be confirmed with the owner before subscribing.
